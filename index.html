Connect Bern — Explore Bern Together

Ten interactive WorkAdventure worlds built around the iconic places of Bern. Wander the city, meet people, play games, and find your way around the community.

🗺️ How to switch between worlds (the .tmj files)

  1. Easiest — use the buttons below. Each card opens one world (one .tmj map) in a new tab. Open several at once to try them simultaneously.
  2. Inside a world — just walk. Every world has glowing exit tiles; step on one to travel to another world. The Connect Bern Hub links to all of them.
  3. By hand in the address bar. A world URL ends in its map file, e.g. …/connectbern.tmj. Swap the filename to load a different world — change connectbern.tmj to library.tmj, aare-float.tmj, etc.
  4. To meet people, share the exact same link: everyone on the same URL lands in the same room.

⚠️ The Play buttons only work once the maps are published to a public host (GitHub Pages). They will not load from localhost because the WorkAdventure play server needs a public URL.
